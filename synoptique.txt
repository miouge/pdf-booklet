    |   ( ) Livret A5  --->  Feuilles A4                                     |
    |                                                                        |
    | Configuration locale : print-medium-pdf.properties                     |
    | Dossier PDF, nom de l'imprimante et chemin de SumatraPDF.              |
    +------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                        |
                                        v
				+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
				| 03  create-booklet.bat                                                 |
				+------------------------------------------------------------------------+

												|
												v
						+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
						| 04  .tools/bin/pdfbook2.cmd                                            |
						+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
						| Lance le moteur avec le Python de .venv.                               |
						+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
															|
															v
								+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
								| 05  pdfbook2/pdfbook2                                                  |
								+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
								| Prepare la generation du livret, sans recadrage.                       |
								+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
																	|
																	v
										+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
										| 06  .tools/bin/pdfjam.cmd                                              |
										+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
										| Lance le script pdfjam via le shell Git Bash.                          |
										+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
																			|
																			v
												+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
												| 07  pdfjam + MiKTeX / pdflatex                                         |
												+------------------------------------------------------------------------+

												+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
																		|
																		v
								+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
								| 08  PDF GENERE  :  <nom-source>-book.pdf                               |

								+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
														|
														v
	+------------------------------------------------------------------------+

											|
